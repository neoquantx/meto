### 1. All 8 Research Gaps — Integration Status

Every single gap from `docs/research.md` is **100% integrated, wired into the UI/API, and unit-tested** (none of the cut-order items were dropped):

1. **Gap 1 — Summaries are too long & nobody reads them (`Integrated`)**
   - **Hard TL;DR cap:** Enforced in `src/lib/verify.ts` (`clipTldr`: max 3 bullets, each $\le 25$ words).
   - **"Who owes what" view:** Rendered in `src/components/meeting-panels.tsx` via `whoOwesWhat()` (`src/lib/meeting-view.ts`), grouping active tasks by person and excluding suggestions, rejected items, and done/dropped items.
   - **Per-person share links & empty state:** `/share/[token]` (`src/lib/share-view.ts` + `src/components/share-document.tsx`) filters to only that person's commitments and transcript lines, and shows *"Nothing for you in this meeting."* when empty.
   - **Timestamp links:** Every commitment, decision, chapter, and ledger event links to its exact timestamp and jumps the player (`seek()`).

2. **Gap 2 — Promises get lost between meetings / Commitment Ledger (`Integrated`)**
   - **Speaker-to-person identity across calls:** `POST /api/meetings/[id]/speakers` (`src/app/api/meetings/[id]/speakers/route.ts`) maps speaker numbers to workspace `people` and assigns `commitments.owner_person_id`.
   - **Automatic Ledger Check:** `runLedgerCheck()` (`src/lib/ledger-check.ts`) runs automatically when speakers are saved (and via the **Re-check ledger** button), sending earlier open/in-progress/changed commitments to Gemini (`src/lib/ledger-model.ts`).
   - **Proof verification & 2-miss "silently dropped" rule:** `verifyLedgerResults()` and `replayLedger()` (`src/lib/ledger.ts`) verify every status quote against the transcript, downgrade unverified quotes to `not_mentioned`, increment `misses` when the owner is present, and mark `misses >= 2` as `dropped`.
   - **Ledger, Brief, & Decisions pages:** `/ledger`, `/brief` (pure DB query, no LLM), and `/decisions` are all live, plus read-only equivalents under `/demo/*`.

3. **Gap 3 — People can't trust AI action items / Evidence-First Extraction (`Integrated`)**
   - **Verbatim quote verification:** `quoteIsSupported()` (`src/lib/verify.ts`) checks normalized substring or $\ge 85\%$ token match across `segment_idx - 1 .. segment_idx + 1`. Unsupported items are dropped and counted in `meetings.dropped_count` (*"N items dropped: no evidence"*).
   - **Code-decided ownership:** `resolveOwner()` (`src/lib/verify.ts`) assigns `self` to the speaker of `segment_idx`, and `assigned` only if a different speaker acknowledges within the next 3 segments (`segment_idx + 1 .. segment_idx + 3`); otherwise `Unassigned`.
   - **Explicit vs. Conditional vs. Suggestion:** Extracted and badged; suggestions are excluded from *Who owes what* and the *Ledger*.
   - **Spoken due dates:** `resolveDueDate()` (`src/lib/verify.ts`) verifies `due_phrase` is inside the quote and parses it with `chrono-node` (`forwardDate: true`) against `recorded_at`.
   - **Human review:** Confirm / Reject buttons on every commitment and decision (`POST /api/meetings/[id]/review`).

4. **Gap 4 — No file upload, weak in-person capture, no Android (`Integrated`)**
   - **Unified capture pipeline:** `/meetings/new` (`src/components/new-meeting-form.tsx`) supports browser mic recording, Chrome tab+mic capture, and `audio/*` or `video/*` file uploads ($\le 50\text{ MB}$, $\le 90\text{ min}$).
   - **Mobile protections:** `navigator.wakeLock.request("screen")` re-acquired on `visibilitychange`, plus the mobile banner: *"Keep this screen on — phones stop web recording when locked."*

5. **Gap 5 — Hinglish code-switching (`Integrated`)**
   - **Deepgram Nova-3 multilingual:** `buildListenUrl()` (`src/lib/deepgram.ts`) uses `model=nova-3`, `language=multi`, `diarize=true`, `diarize_model=latest`, `utterances=true`, `smart_format=true`, `mip_opt_out=true`, and `keyterm` from the user's glossary (with automatic retry without `keyterm` if rejected).
   - **Summary language picker:** English (`en`), Hindi (`hi`), and Hinglish (`hinglish`) per meeting and in `/settings`.
   - **Devanagari-safe normalization:** `normalizeText()` and `simpleTokens()` preserve Unicode combining marks (`\p{M}`) so Hindi vowel signs (`matras`) stay intact.

Reloading mid-recording shows `RecoverBanner` (`src/components/recover-banner.tsx`) to rebuild and upload the blob; local chunks are deleted only after `/api/meetings/[id]/uploaded` succeeds.
   - **Live mic meter & 30s silence warning:** `AnalyserNode` RMS meter in `useMeetingRecorder` warns if audio stays near-silent for 30 seconds; `beforeunload` warns before closing the tab.
